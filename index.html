How it runs

From first call to handover

  1. 01

    Brief & site visit

    We walk the site, take the constraints seriously, and agree the scope and budget range before drawing anything.

  2. 02

    Concept & 3D

    Layouts and massing, then renders. You approve the look before we spend time on documentation.

  3. 03

    Drawings & BOQ

    Full construction and interior packages, with a measured BOQ so contractors bid on identical scope.

  4. 04

    Tender & site follow-up

    We compare bids, flag the padded lines, and stay on the project while it is built.
