01 — The method

From drawing to bill, in five moves.

01Drawing
  1. 01

    Read the drawings

    Structural DWG, vector PDF or a scanned set. Grids, levels, column and beam schedules are pulled out first — and every assumption is written down before a single element is placed.

  2. 02

    Build the concrete

    Footings, raft, necks, columns, walls, beams, slabs and stairs modelled at true size and joined, so nothing is counted twice where elements meet.

  3. 03

    Place every bar

    Reinforcement follows the structural details: laps, cranks, starter bars, cantilever U-bars, top mesh and ties — checked so no bar sits outside its concrete.

  4. 04

    Schedule it

    Concrete by element and floor, steel by diameter, blockwork by wall type. Bar-bending schedules to BS 8666. The model is the single source of truth.

  5. 05

    Deliver the workbook

    A bilingual client workbook — summary, concrete, steel and one sheet per element — print-ready, with automated checks so every total ties back to the model.
